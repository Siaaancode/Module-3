Our Menu .

Welcome to our menu! We offer a variety of delicious dishes inspired by traditional Japanese cuisine.

Appetizers

  • Edamame - Steamed soybeans with sea salt
  • Gyoza - Pan-fried dumplings filled with pork and vegetables
  • Tempura - Lightly battered and fried vegetables or seafood

Main Courses

  • Teriyaki Chicken - Grilled chicken glazed with teriyaki sauce
  • Salmon Teriyaki - Baked salmon with teriyaki glaze
  • Beef Yakiniku - Grilled beef with soy-based marinade

Desserts

  • Green Tea Ice Cream - Creamy ice cream infused with matcha
  • Mochi - Chewy rice cakes filled with sweetened bean paste
  • Profiteroles - Light pastry puffs filled with vanilla cream and topped with chocolate sauce

Important Information!

All our menu items contain allergens. Please inform your server of any dietary restrictions.
